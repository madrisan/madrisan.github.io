The version 56 of *Open Scores for Piano* ("*Milestone 2*") is available!
The target of *Milestone 2* was to digitize J.S. Bach's *Suites Anglaises*, and with this
release the whole set (BWV806-811) is now complete.

Open Scores for Piano is a collection of piano sheet music and piano transcriptions, engraved with
[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/) and distributed with the full source code, under the
[CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.html) license.

### What’s new in this release

#### ADDED

 * J.S. Bach: Suite Anglaise 6 BWV811

#### CHANGED

 * J.S. Bach: Fuga C-Dur BWV952 - minor changes
 * J.S. Bach: Chromatische Fantasie und Fugue - major updates particularly for hand positions
 * J.S. Bach: Das wohltemperierte Klavier – add a simple index with pdf links to pages

#### FIXED

 * J.S. Bach: Suite Anglaise 2 BWV807 - minor fixes
 * J.S. Bach: Die Kunst der Fuge (BWV1080) - fix a typo

Full Changelog: [`v55...v56`](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/compare/v55..v56)

The project and its LilyPond sources are available at [GitHub](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores),
and the updated PDF scores can be downloaded from the
[v56 release page](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/releases/tag/v56).
