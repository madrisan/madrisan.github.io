The version 66 of *Open Scores for Piano* is available!

Open Scores for Piano is a collection of piano sheet music and piano transcriptions, engraved with
[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/) and distributed with the full source code, under the
[CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.html) license.

### What’s new in this release

#### ADDED

 * J.S. Bach: Fuga A-Dur BWV950
 * Andrea Turchetto: Canon in F sharp minor
 * Andrea Turchetto: Fugue on "The final countdown"
 * Andrea Turchetto: Canone cromatico

#### FIXED

 * Pietro Domenico Paradisi: Toccata in La Maggiore (P 893.06) - missing sharp alteration

Full Changelog: [`v65...v66`](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/compare/v65..v66)

The project and its LilyPond sources are available at [GitHub](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores),
and the updated PDF scores can be downloaded from the
[v66 release page](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/releases/tag/v66).
