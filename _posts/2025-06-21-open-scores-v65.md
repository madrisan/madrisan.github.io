The version 65 of *Open Scores for Piano* is available!

Open Scores for Piano is a collection of piano sheet music and piano transcriptions, engraved with
[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/) and distributed with the full source code, under the
[CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.html) license.

### What’s new in this release

#### ADDED

 * Pietro Domenico Paradisi: Toccata in La Maggiore (P 893.06)

#### CHANGED

 * J.S. Bach: Die Kunst der Fuge (BWV1080) - annotations for fugues I-VIII
 * J.S. Bach: Die Kunst der Fuge (BWV1080) - translate notes on contrapunctus XIV in italian
 * J.S. Bach: Die Kunst der Fuge (BWV1080) - Contrapunctus I-II: better repartition of notes between hands

Full Changelog: [`v64...v65`](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/compare/v64..v65)

The project and its LilyPond sources are available at [GitHub](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores),
and the updated PDF scores can be downloaded from the
[v65 release page](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/releases/tag/v65).
