The version 50 of *Open Scores for Piano* ("*Milestone 1*") is available!
The target of *Milestone 1* was to digitize all volumes of J.S. Bach's *Clavierübung*, except
the third one, which contains works for organ, and with this release volumes I, II and IV are
now complete.

Open Scores for Piano is a collection of piano sheet music and piano transcriptions, engraved with
[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/) and distributed with the full source code, under the
[CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.html) license.

### What’s new in this release

#### ADDED

 * J.S. Bach: Ouverture nach Französischer Art (BWV831)
 * J.S. Bach: Praeludium und Fughetta in d-moll (BWV899)
 * J.S. Bach: Praeludium und Fughetta in e-moll (BWV900)
 * Bach/Kempff: Siciliano (BWV1031)
 * Bach/Kempff: Largo from Harpsichord Concerto BWV1056

Full Changelog: [`v49...v50`](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/compare/v49..v50)

and the updated PDF scores can be downloaded from the
