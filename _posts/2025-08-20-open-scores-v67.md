The version 67 of *Open Scores for Piano* is available!

Open Scores for Piano is a collection of piano sheet music and piano transcriptions, engraved with
[LilyPond](https://lilypond.org/) and distributed with the full source code, under the
[CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://spdx.org/licenses/CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.html) license.

### What’s new in this release

#### ADDED

 * J.S. Bach: Fuga C-Dur BWV946
 * J.S. Bach: Organ Concerto (Largo) BWV596
 * J.S. Bach: Präludium und Fuge über den Namen BACH BWV898
 * Johann Christian Bach: Sonata op.17 n.5 W.A11

#### FIXED

 * J.S. Bach: Die Kunst der Fuge (BWV1080) - fix typo in front cover

Full Changelog: [`v66...v67`](https://github.com/madrisan/open-scores/compare/v66..v67)
